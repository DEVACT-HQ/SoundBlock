## Privacy notice

SoundBlock has no account, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and Devact collects nothing about you. The only data that leaves your PC is what any internet connection shows when SoundBlock checks GitHub for updates, and you can switch that off.

### Who is responsible

Devact, at the address and email above.

### What stays on your PC

SoundBlock keeps these on your PC, where Devact cannot see them:

- Your rules, which name apps and audio devices, in `%APPDATA%\SoundBlock\rules.txt`.
- A small file in the same folder, if you switch update checks off.
- A Windows autostart entry, if you turn that on.

SoundBlock does not record or send audio. It only reads how loud each app is, to light up its wire in the window.

### Update checks

30 seconds after it starts and then every 6 hours, SoundBlock fetches a small file from GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`) that names the latest version. A new version is only downloaded, from the same place, after you click **Update**.

Like any request on the internet, this shows GitHub your IP address and the time of the request. Devact does not receive this information. GitHub only shows Devact total download numbers, which do not identify anyone.

The purpose is to tell you about new versions, including security fixes. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in keeping SoundBlock safe and working (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

To switch this off, right-click the SoundBlock icon near the clock and turn off **Check for updates**. SoundBlock then does not contact the internet at all, but it also cannot tell you about security fixes.
